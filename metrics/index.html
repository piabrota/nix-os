Processes, memory, vCPU

One card for each NixOS host. The numbers come from /proc, every 10 seconds; each site is asked for an answer every minute. A host is offline when its data is older than 30 seconds.

CPU % of a process is a share of one vCPU. Rates and CPU % are averages over the last interval; pressure is the share of the last 10 s that tasks stalled (some / full). The lists show 10 processes; a limit shows only for units with a MemoryMax. The inventory is what the deployed configuration declares, counted at build time. A site is up when it answers below HTTP 500. A visit is a page that a browser got in the last 24 h, a visitor is an address; bots are not counted. For machines: all.json (every host) and /metrics (Prometheus).
